When it comes to summer camps, there is certainly no shortage of options. From the YMCA to local churches to university-sponsored youth camps, kids could be in a different place every week of the summer. With all of that competition, what sets camps at Bodies in Motion apart? What makes us special?

## Unique Camps

Our camps are themed, and the themes change from year to year. Over the years our lineup has included Ninja, Secret Agent, Treasure Hunters, Hollywood, Splash, Ninja Warrior, Adventure, Myth Busters, Super Hero, Kid vs. Wild and Bugs camps.

Where else can you find such a wide variety of experiences?! Even when a popular camp comes back, each version has its own unique twist, so no two weeks are ever the same. For that matter, no two summers are the same, since camps are updated and changed out every year.

## Complete Care

Our [camps](/camps) are for kids ages 4 to 12, and each camp lists its own age range when you register. Camp runs from 9:00 am to 3:00 pm, with optional before-camp care starting at 7:30 am and after-camp care until 6:00 pm. You can rest easy knowing that your child is well-cared for, regardless of your crazy work schedule. Speaking of being well-cared for, optional lunch can be added when you register if your mornings are rushed. And let’s face it, when was the last time you *didn’t* have a rushed morning?

## Skilled Staff

At Bodies in Motion, employees are referred to as “Motioneers.” They are the people who get guests moving, thus creating motion. Every camper is guided by Motioneers who are childcare certified, background checked, and trained in CPR and first aid. This ensures that every camper has the best possible experience while staying safe.

## Camps All Year Long

Camps are not only for summer. We also run one-day camps on no-school days, plus holiday and break camps such as Spring Break and Thanksgiving-week camp days. Not sure when school breaks fit? Our guide on [whether camps are only for summer](/blog/are-camps-only-for-summer) explains what to expect.

## Summer Camp Central

Bodies in Motion is summer camp central. With such a wide variety of mentally and physically stimulating camps, we keep kids moving, learning, and growing all summer long. Forget the “I’m boooored… there’s nothing to dooooo” moans and groans.
