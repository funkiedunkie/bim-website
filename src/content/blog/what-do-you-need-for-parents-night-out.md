When you think of a typical afternoon, you think of coming home and playing with your children. A typical night might involve more playing and getting them ready for sleep. However, Parents’ Night Out allows for you to have a well-deserved date. Drop your kids off at the Bodies in Motion gym for a night of fun while you and your partner go out, run errands, or have a date at home.

When presented with this service, most parents ask, “What do I need to do or to have for Parents’ Night Out?” Today, we’re going into that as well as what you can expect when you let your child attend a [Parents’ Night Out](/parents-night-out) at Bodies in Motion in Boise, Idaho.

## What Does Your Child Need for Parents’ Night Out?

When you take your child to our Parents’ Night Out, you’ll drop them off at 5:00 pm and pick them back up at 9:00 pm. You don’t have to pack anything for them! Dinner is included, and fun themes and games will have them running and playing all night long.

Each event has its own theme, and we announce dates on our [Parents’ Night Out page](/parents-night-out). If your child wants, dressing up to match the theme is part of the fun, but we understand if it isn’t something your ten-year-old wants to do with their evening.

## What Do You Need for Your Parents’ Night Out?

After you register, there is nothing else you need to do! Now, it’s all about what you want. Prepare for your night, whether that is a date, a movie, shopping, or just sitting at home in silence. Here is a quick recap of your options.

- **Run errands:** Many of our parents love to use this time to catch up with errands and shopping. Whether it’s groceries, gifts, or just a shopping trip, you’ll have four hours to get it done.
- **Date night:** If you are looking to have fun with your significant other, then Parents’ Night Out will ensure that you are free from children to do so. Head to the movies, a dinner, or just stay at home.
- **Relax:** You don’t have to do anything for Parents’ Night Out if you don’t want to. Many of our parents are known for going home and relaxing for the four hours of the event. This gives them a chance for a spa night while no one is home.

## What Happens at Parents’ Night Out?

Each Parents’ Night Out is packed with games and activities that follow the night’s theme, plus dinner. For example, our Cosmic Glow Party includes a Cosmic Silent Disco, a Glow Scavenger Hunt, Cosmic Ring Toss, pizza and a sweet treat. Kids have four hours to explore our gym and take part in the activities we’ve planned.

## Who Can Come?

The Cosmic Glow Party is for ages 5 to 12. We also offer Family Night Out, for ages 3 (potty trained) up to adults, so the whole family can laugh, play and make memories together. Spaces are limited, so register ahead of time.

If you are looking for a Parents’ Night Out, [see our Parents’ Night Out page](/parents-night-out) for the latest event or [contact us](/contact). We are in Boise, Idaho, and our events run from 5 to 9 at night.
